# Data provenance and third-party notices

DigitalMouseCA1 code is distributed under the BSD-3-Clause license. Source
datasets retain their original licenses and citation requirements.

| Component | Source | Use in this model | Redistribution policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mouse CA1 geometry | Kim mouse brain atlas, 20-µm annotation | SO, SP, SR and SLM geometry; intrinsic coordinates | Confirm the atlas terms before depositing raw or derived volumes |
| Soma and transcriptomic identity | Allen Brain Cell Atlas, Zhuang MERFISH series | Mouse CA1 soma positions and transcriptomic mapping | Distribute a downloader and provenance manifest unless direct redistribution is confirmed |
| Morphology and e-model priors | Romani et al. (2024), DOI 10.1371/journal.pbio.3002861; data DOI 10.7910/DVN/TN3DUI | CA1 morphology/e-model priors | Dataset recorded locally as CC BY 4.0; preserve attribution |
| Recurrent physiology prior | Ecker et al. (2020), DOI 10.1002/hipo.23220 | Pathway-specific synaptic parameters | Model-derived parameter tables must cite the source |

The distributed model is a mouse--rat hybrid: geometry and soma/transcriptomic
information are mouse derived, whereas morphology and electrophysiology priors
are predominantly rat derived. It is a research model, not a clinical device.
